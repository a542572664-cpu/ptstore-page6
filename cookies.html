Control and Transparency

Cookie Policy

This policy explains which technologies EIIiO STORE uses, what they do, and how you can accept, reject, or change your preferences.

1. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small text files that a website or third party stores in your browser. They allow information to be remembered during a session or between visits. Similar technologies, such as local storage and pixels, may also store identifiers, remember preferences, or communicate information about a visit.

This website uses local storage to remember your privacy choice and may load Meta Pixel and its marketing cookies only with your consent.

2. Necessary Cookies and Storage

Strictly necessary functions are always active so the website can remember whether you accepted or rejected marketing. EIIiO STORE stores the eiio_cookie_consent_v1 preference in localStorage. It is not a cookie and does not, by itself, transmit information to EIIiO STORE or Meta.

Rejecting marketing does not prevent you from viewing pages or images or using WhatsApp links and buttons.

3. Marketing Cookies

Marketing cookies are disabled by default. They may be used only after you actively allow them by selecting “Accept All” or enabling “Marketing” and saving your settings.

If you allow them, Meta may place or read identifiers to recognize a browser, measure campaigns, attribute visits, and show more relevant advertising. You may withdraw your consent at any time.

4. Purpose of Meta Pixel

Meta Pixel (ID: 1364547125062669) helps measure visits and ad performance, create audiences, and personalize advertising across Meta products. It may send Meta information such as the visited URL, date and time, IP address, browser, device, and cookie identifiers.

Meta Pixel code is not downloaded before marketing consent is given. If you reject marketing, this website does not send PageView, Contact, or other events to Meta. Each page load generates no more than one PageView event, and only after consent has been given.

Learn more in Meta's Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy.

5. GitHub Pages: Hosting and Technical Logs

This website is published through GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs and retains the IP addresses of visitors to GitHub Pages websites for security purposes, even if the visitor is not signed in. GitHub manages these technical logs, and they do not depend on your Meta Pixel choice.

See the GitHub Pages documentation, the Privacy Statement, and GitHub's cookie information.

6. How Long Cookies and Preferences Last

Technology Provider and Category Purpose Stated Duration

_fbp Meta — marketing Identify browsers for advertising and analytics. 90 days, according to Meta's policy.

Meta may use other cookies depending on your session, account, device, or settings. Their names and retention periods may change; review Meta's current policy for details. Session cookies disappear when you close your browser, while persistent cookies remain until they expire or are deleted.

7. How to Accept, Reject, or Withdraw Consent

On your first visit, you can choose “Accept All,” “Reject All,” or “Customize.” Marketing is turned off by default. Accepting and rejecting are equally easy, and rejecting does not limit the website's main features.

To withdraw consent, open Cookie Settings, turn off “Marketing,” and save, or select “Reject All.” From that point on, the website stops sending new Meta Pixel events. Withdrawing consent does not automatically remove cookies Meta has already placed; you can delete them through your browser settings.

8. How to Reopen Cookie Settings

The Cookie Settings link is available in the footer of every page. You can use it at any time to review, change, or withdraw your choice. If you clear your browser storage, the website will ask for your decision again on your next visit.

9. Third-Party Services

  • Meta: Meta Pixel loads only with marketing consent. Meta may use cookies and identifiers under its own policies.
  • WhatsApp: The buttons are links. WhatsApp receives information when you choose to open or use its service, in accordance with its Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy.
  • PayPal: if you make a payment, you enter PayPal's environment, which applies its Privacy Statement and its own technologies.
  • GitHub: hosts the website through GitHub Pages and manages technical logs and technologies under its policies.

Preferences saved on this website do not control cookies that these third parties may set when you visit their websites directly or use their applications.
